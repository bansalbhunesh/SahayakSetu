## Design Context

### Users
**Primary**: Rural and semi-urban Indian citizens with low digital literacy. Using on basic Android phones, often in regional language. May not read well. Voice is their natural interface — they tap first, read second.
**Secondary**: Urban family members or village-level agents helping others navigate schemes.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Trusted. Simple. Empowering.
The product is "a trusted government assistant that speaks your language and guides you step-by-step."
Reference: DigiLocker (government-grade trust, clarity) × Google Assistant (conversational ease, immediate action).
NOT: startup SaaS, developer tool, or fintech dark-mode.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light by default (trust, clarity, government feel). Dark mode available via toggle.
- **Primary colour**: Deep Blue `#0B3D91` — authority, government, trust. Structural colour.
- **Accent**: Saffron `#F07B28` — used SPARINGLY for CTAs and active states only. Not backgrounds.
- **Background**: White / very lightly blue-tinted neutral.
- **Success**: Green `#2E7D32`.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dark mode, neon on dark, purple-blue gradient AI aesthetic.

### Design Principles
1. **Voice is the hero** — the mic button must be the largest, most prominent interactive element on the page. Everything else supports it.
2. **Touch targets for real hands** — language pills, buttons, chips must be at least 44px tall. Low-literacy users tap by shape and position, not by reading.
3. **Blue builds trust, saffron calls to action** — deep blue for structure and headings, saffron only for the one action you want them to take.
4. **Guidance over information** — surface next steps, not raw data. Answers should feel like a human guide speaking, not a database result.
5. **Bilingual hierarchy is intentional** — Hindi/regional script should carry equal visual weight to English, never feel like a footnote.
